Main Users

The system is designed for three major users.

Student

Students can build profiles, view job openings, apply for jobs, and track applications.

Admin

Admins can manage students, post opportunities, verify records, and monitor placements.

Company

Companies can post jobs, view student applicants, and shortlist suitable candidates.
